Privacy policy

for the app “Survival Prototype” (test version) and this website · Last updated: November 2026

In short: The game works without an account, without an internet connection and without servers of our own. It contains no ads, no purchases and no usage analytics. Your progress stays on your device. We do not receive any personal data from you. Data is processed only by Google when you download the app from Google Play or join a test.

2. Data on your device

The app stores your progress (e.g. inventory, storage, buildings, progress) and your settings (e.g. graphics level) only in the app storage on your device. This data is not transferred to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by choosing “Neues Spiel” (new game) in the game, uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the Android settings.

The app does not connect to any servers while you play. It does not use the advertising ID, location services, the camera or the microphone.

3. Google Play and test programs

The app is offered through Google Play. When you download or update it, Google processes data under its own responsibility, e.g. your Google account and device information. We only receive anonymous statistics (e.g. number of installs, crash reports without personal reference).

If you join a test, we can see in the Google Play Console the email address with which you were added to the tester list, or your membership in the test group. We use it only to give you access to the test version and remove it after the test (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you send us feedback by email, we use your address and message only to read it and reply if needed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in improving the game).

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

4. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect it against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The site uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no third-party content. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Children

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect data from children.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As we do not receive personal data from the app, please contact Google directly regarding data held by Google; we are happy to help at littlelegiongames@proton.me.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

7. Changes

If new features are added (e.g. optional reward videos, purchases or usage analytics), we will update this policy before the feature appears in the app. The version published here applies.
