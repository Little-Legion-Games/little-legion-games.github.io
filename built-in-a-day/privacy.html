Privacy Policy

for the app “Built in a Day: Idle Roman Empire” and this website · Last updated: October 2026

In short: The game works without an account and without our own servers. Your progress stays on your device. We do not receive any personal data from you. Data only goes to Google when you download the app from Google Play and – in later versions – voluntarily watch an ad video, make a purchase or join leaderboards.

2. Data on your device

The app stores your game progress and settings (e.g. sound, music, day and night) only in the app storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the Android settings.

Notifications: If you allow it, the app schedules a reminder on your device (e.g. “The storehouses are full”). No data is transmitted. You can revoke the permission at any time in the Android settings.

Feedback by email: If you choose "Send feedback" in the game, your email app opens with your answers plus app version, game progress (era, play time, provinces) and Android version. Nothing is sent until you tap send in your email app. We then receive your email address and the content of the message. We use both only to read your feedback and reply if needed, and delete the message once it is no longer needed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in improving the game).

3. Google Play

The app is distributed via Google Play. When you download, update or buy, Google processes data under its own responsibility, such as your Google account, device information and payment details. We only receive anonymous statistics (e.g. number of installs, crash reports without personal data). More information: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Services added in later versions

The following features are introduced step by step. They are only active if they are included in your app version. Before introducing them, we review and update this policy.

a) Optional rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short ad video to receive a reward. There are no ads that you do not start yourself. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The following may be processed: your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. We only request non-personalised ads; Google then uses the data mainly to deliver ads, limit how often they are shown and prevent fraud.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG), which Google's consent dialog asks for before you watch your first video. You can change or withdraw it at any time in the game settings under "Privacy settings for videos". Transfers to the USA (Google LLC) are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More information: How Google uses data for advertising.

b) In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; only Google sees your payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases belong to your Google account and unlocks them on your device – also after reinstalling. We run no server of our own for this and receive no receipts or account data. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). More information: Google Privacy Policy.

c) Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your player name and weekly fame are sent to Google and shown on a leaderboard. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can sign out at any time.

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The site uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no third-party content. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children

The game is intended for all ages and contains historical content. We do not collect any data from children ourselves. Ads are only requested as non-personalised ads. Children and teenagers should only make purchases with their parents' permission; Google Play lets parents require approval for purchases.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. As we do not store any personal data ourselves, please contact Google directly about data held by Google; we are happy to help at benediktgeitner@gmx.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

8. Changes

When new features are added or services change, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
